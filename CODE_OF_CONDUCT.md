# 行为准则 / Code of Conduct

## 我们的承诺 / Our Pledge

我们致力于为所有人提供友好、安全、开放的参与体验，无论年龄、体型、残疾、种族、性别认同与表达、经验水平、教育程度、社会经济地位、国籍、外貌、种族、宗教或性取向。

We are committed to making participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## 我们的标准 / Standards

有助于建设积极环境的行为包括：

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- 用友善和善的语言待人 / Being kind and welcoming
- 尊重不同的观点与经验 / Respecting differing viewpoints and experiences
- 优雅地接受并建设性地给出批评 / Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- 关注对社区最有利的事情 / Focusing on what is best for the community

不可接受的行为包括：

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- 性暗示的言语或图像，以及不受欢迎的性关注 / Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
- 恶意评论、人身攻击、政治攻击 / Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, personal or political attacks
- 公开或私下的骚扰 / Public or private harassment
- 未经许可发布他人的私人信息 / Publishing others' private information without permission

## 执行 / Enforcement

违反行为准则的行为可通过 [GitHub Security Advisories](../../security/advisories/new)（选择 "Report a vulnerability" 或自定义报告）私下向维护者举报。所有举报将被保密评估与处理。

Reports of unacceptable behavior can be made privately to the maintainers via [GitHub Security Advisories](../../security/advisories/new). All reports will be reviewed and handled confidentially.

维护者有责任阐明并执行本行为准则，对违规行为采取包括警告、临时或永久封禁等适当措施。

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this standard, and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior they deem inappropriate.
